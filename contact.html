Contact Teacup Puzzlers

By the Teacup Puzzlers Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

We read every message ourselves, and nothing brightens our day like a note from a fellow puzzler. The best way to reach us is by email — there is no form to wrestle with and no account to create.

Write to us

Send your note to hello@teacuppuzzlers.com. Tell us which puzzle you were enjoying and what is on your mind, and we will do our best to help. We aim to reply within two business days.

If you believe a trivia answer is wrong, our team would be grateful to hear at answers@teacuppuzzlers.com. We check our facts with care, but we are human, and we will gladly correct any genuine error.

A few friendly questions

Is Teacup Puzzlers free?
Entirely. Every game is free to play, there is nothing to buy, and we take no payments and run no advertising.
Do I need to sign up?
Not at all. Just open a page and start playing. We ask for nothing and keep nothing about you beyond your text-size choice, which stays on your own device.
Can I print the puzzles?
Yes — our Printables page offers puzzle sheets with answer keys, ready for pencil and paper, and most pages have a Print button too.
The games won't load. What's wrong?
The interactive games need JavaScript switched on in your browser. If it is off, the printable sheets work beautifully without it, and all the written guides remain fully readable.
Will there be new puzzles?
Today's Puzzle refreshes every day, and we add new games and trivia over time. Reader suggestions often shape what we make next, so do send yours.
