Cookie Policy

By the Teacup Puzzlers Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

Effective date: October 10, 2026. This cookie policy covers everything published on the Teacup Puzzlers website.

Teacup Puzzlers uses no tracking cookies and no advertising cookies. This page explains the two small things it does store, both on your own device.

What we store

Rather than cookies, Teacup Puzzlers uses your browser's local storage to remember two choices:

  • Your text size — so the A / A+ / A++ setting stays the way you left it.
  • The storage notice — so the small bar at the bottom does not reappear once you dismiss it.

Both values live on your device, are readable only by your browser, and are never sent to Teacup Puzzlers or to anyone else.

What we do not use

Teacup Puzzlers runs no analytics, no ad network, and no cross-site tracking. No one is building a profile of you here.

What “local storage” means

Local storage is a small notepad that your own web browser keeps on your computer, tablet, or phone. A website such as Teacup Puzzlers can jot a short note there — in our case, two small preferences — and read it back on your next visit. Unlike some cookies, these notes are never automatically sent back to a server, so they cannot be used to follow you from site to site.

Do Not Track and privacy settings

Because Teacup Puzzlers does no tracking in the first place, there is nothing for a browser's “Do Not Track” setting to switch off here. You are already as private on this site as that setting could ever make you.

Managing what is stored

You can clear the two stored values at any time by clearing your browser's site data for teacuppuzzlers.com, or by using your browser's private-browsing mode. Clearing them simply resets the text size to normal and brings back the notice bar.

In short, nothing you do on Teacup Puzzlers is recorded for marketing, sold to anyone, or used to follow you elsewhere. The two small preferences exist only to make the site more comfortable for you, and they stay with you.

We take this plain approach on Teacup Puzzlers because the simplest way to protect your privacy is to gather almost nothing in the first place. There is no profile of you here to lose, leak, or sell, and that is exactly how we intend to keep it.

If this ever changes

If Teacup Puzzlers ever adds analytics or advertising, we will update this page and the notice bar to describe it before it goes live.

Contact us

Questions about storage can go to hello@teacuppuzzlers.com.
